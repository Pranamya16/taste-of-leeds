# Code of Conduct

We want Taste of Leeds to be a welcoming space for people with different backgrounds, experiences and opinions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss food and culture in good faith.
- Accept that people may have different regional and personal experiences.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech or discriminatory language.
- Threats, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Deliberate spam, impersonation or fraudulent reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that conflicts with these expectations, contact the site owner or use the reporting method provided on the website. Reports should be handled discreetly and fairly.
