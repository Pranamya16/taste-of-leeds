# Content and moderation guidelines

Taste of Leeds is intended to be welcoming and respectful.

## Writing about cultural food

- Describe your own experience rather than speaking for an entire culture.
- Recognise that cuisines vary by region, family, community and personal tradition.
- Explain why you recommend a dish or place.
- Avoid treating one recipe or preparation as the only correct version.
- Do not use cultural background as a proxy for expertise; let contributors explain their own experience.

## Reviews should

- Be based on a genuine visit or clearly identified first-hand experience.
- Focus on food, service and relevant visitor information.
- Be specific and constructive.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest, such as a personal or business relationship.

## Do not publish

- Hate speech, harassment, threats or discriminatory content.
- Private personal information.
- Fake reviews, spam or undisclosed paid promotion.
- Unverified allegations about individuals or businesses.
- Photos or text copied without permission.

## Photos and business information

- Use your own photos or images you have permission to use.
- Credit creators where required.
- Verify addresses, links and opening hours before publication.
- Provide a way for a business or contributor to request a correction.

## Moderation process

1. New submissions are marked pending.
2. A site moderator checks relevance, tone, privacy and basic factual claims.
3. The moderator publishes, requests edits or rejects the submission.
4. Users can report content that may breach these guidelines.
5. Reports and correction requests should be reviewed promptly.

These guidelines are an initial product policy, not legal advice. Before public launch, prepare suitable privacy, terms and content policies for the service and its jurisdiction.
