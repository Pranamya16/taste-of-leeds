# Project brief

## 1. Overview

Taste of Leeds is a community-led food discovery website for Leeds, UK. It helps people find food spots through cuisine, dishes, local knowledge and first-hand experiences.

## 2. Problem

People often discover restaurants through generic ratings and search results. Those sources may not explain regional food traditions, dishes worth trying, or why a place matters to people familiar with a cuisine.

## 3. Product vision

Make it easier to discover Leeds food spots while giving communities space to share their own food knowledge and experiences.

## 4. Intended audiences

- Leeds residents who enjoy exploring food.
- Students and people new to Leeds.
- People looking for food from a particular country or region.
- People who want to share recommendations and food stories.
- Independent food businesses interested in being discovered.

## 5. First-version scope

The first public version should prioritise:
- A welcoming homepage.
- A searchable/browsable list of food spots.
- Cuisine and neighbourhood filters.
- Individual food-spot pages.
- A recommendation submission form.
- A simple review/story format.
- Manual approval of submitted content.

## 6. Not in the first version

To keep the first build manageable, postpone:
- Native mobile apps.
- Direct restaurant bookings or payments.
- Private messaging.
- Complex badges, leaderboards or gamification.
- Automated authenticity ratings.
- Restaurant-owner dashboards.
- AI-generated authenticity judgements.

## 7. Success measures

Early indicators could include:
- Number of verified food-spot profiles.
- Number of cuisines and neighbourhoods represented.
- Number of useful community recommendations.
- Percentage of submissions that can be approved.
- Feedback from early visitors about whether the site helps them discover somewhere new.

## 8. Product principles

- Community perspectives over a single authority.
- Explain context rather than assigning an authenticity score.
- Accurate, transparent and up-to-date information.
- Accessible, mobile-friendly design.
- Respectful representation of cultures and cuisines.
